p5.mbt demos

Sketches written in MoonBit against p5.mbt, a port of p5.js. Build them from the repository root with moon build --target js --release and serve the root directory.

Films

Five Thousand Years of AsiaA two-minute WEBGL film in 13 chapters, from the Indus cities to the Shinkansen.
A Short History of Computation104 seconds from the Antikythera mechanism to Processing, drawn in 2D.

3D

galaxy12,000 stars in two spiral arms, per-vertex colors, drag to orbit.
lorenzThe Lorenz attractor traced as a 3D line colored by speed.
orreryLights, emissive and specular materials, generated textures, font-shader labels.
terrainNoise terrain from triangle strips, with fog added to p5's material shader by a hook.

Shaders and buffers

mandelbrotAn endless zoom computed in a custom fragment shader.
feedbackTwo framebuffers feeding each other: zoomed, turned and faded trails.

2D

boidsFlocking from separation, alignment and cohesion. The mouse attracts.
particlesWords from text_to_points, each point a spring. Move the mouse.
treeA recursive tree swaying in Perlin-noise wind, in blossom.
